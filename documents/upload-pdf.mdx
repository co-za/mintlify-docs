This guide walks you through uploading a PDF document and getting it in front of the right people. In Policy Passport, documents are assigned to **groups** — every active user in an assigned group automatically receives the document when it's published.

## Before you start

- You must have **administrator access** to your Policy Passport dashboard. If you can't see the **Files** section in the sidebar, contact your account owner to check your permissions.
- Your PDF must be **under 25 MB** and saved in `.pdf` format. Other file types are not supported.

---

## Step 1: Start a new file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Files section">
    From your admin dashboard, click **Files** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new file">
    Click **New File** in the top-right corner. Enter a descriptive name for your document and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select File Upload">
    On the content type screen, select **File Upload**.

    > *"Use this if you want to upload a file, such as a PDF document."*

    Then click **Next** to proceed to the upload screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 2: Upload your PDF

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your file">
    On the **File Upload** screen, either:
    - Drag and drop your PDF onto the upload area, or
    - Click **Choose a file** to browse your computer.

    Only one file can be uploaded per document. Once you select a file, the upload begins automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the upload to complete">
    A progress bar shows the upload percentage. Once the upload is successful, the filename appears and the **Next** button becomes available.

    <Warning>
      If the upload fails, check that your file is a valid `.pdf` and is under **25 MB**. Files in other formats (Word, PowerPoint, etc.) will be rejected.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next">
    Click **Next** to move on to version and settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 3: Set version and options

This step captures the key metadata for your document.

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **File Version** | Yes | A version label (e.g. `1.0`, `Jan 2025`). Required before the document can be published. |
| **Category** | No | Assign the document to a category for easier organisation. |
| **Days to Complete** | No | Set a deadline (in days) for users to read and complete the document after it is assigned. |
| **Disable Download and Printing** | No | When checked, users can only view the document inside Policy Passport — they cannot save or print a copy. |
| **Do Not Embed PDF** | No | Shows a download link instead of displaying the PDF inline in the browser. |
| **Lock Document** | No | Prevents other administrators from editing this document. |

<Tip>
  **File Version** is the only required field in this step. You will not be able to publish the document without it.
</Tip>

Click **Next** once you're done.

---

## Step 4: Add notes and review details (optional)

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Display Note">
    Check **Display Note?** to write a message that users see before they open the document. Use this to highlight important context, changes from a previous version, or actions required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Administrative Review Date">
    Set a date to remind yourself when this document is due for review. Enable **Advanced Reminders** to receive email nudges at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days before the review date, and then daily until actioned.

    You can also add **Reminder Subscribers** — other admins who will receive these reminder emails alongside you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Document Owner / Approved By / Date Approved">
    Optional fields for recording document ownership and approval history. Visible to administrators only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CPD Details">
    If your organisation tracks Continuing Professional Development (CPD), fill in all five fields: **CPD Name**, **CPD Points**, **CPD Hours**, **CPD Accreditation Number**, and **CPD Accreditation Body**. These fields work together — if you fill in one, all five are required.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Click **Next** to continue.

---

## Step 5: Add a test (optional)

Attach a quiz to verify that users have understood the document content.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose whether to add a test">
    Select **Yes** to create a test, or **No** to skip this step entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of questions">
    Enter how many questions users must answer (between 1 and 10). Policy Passport randomly selects from your full question pool, so you can write more questions than this number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your questions">
    After clicking **Next**, you'll be taken to the question builder to write each question and its multiple-choice answer options.

    <Tip>
      If your organisation has **AI Question Generation** enabled, click **Generate Questions Automatically** and Policy Passport will analyse your PDF and suggest questions. This typically takes 1–2 minutes for longer documents.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 6: Set acknowledgement and feedback (optional)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Require an acknowledgement">
    Check **Acknowledgement Required Upon Completion?** to ask users to sign off once they've read the document. A default acknowledgement statement is pre-filled — edit it to match your organisation's language if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Require feedback">
    Check **Feedback Required Upon Completion?** and enter a prompt (e.g. *"Do you have any questions about this policy?"*). Users will see this prompt and can submit a free-text response after completing the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to finish the setup wizard. Your document is now created and saved in draft status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 7: Assign the document to groups

Documents in Policy Passport are assigned to **groups**, not to individual users. All active users in an assigned group automatically receive the document when it is published.

<Info>
  It is not possible to assign a document to individual users directly. If you need to assign to a specific person, add them to a group first, then assign the document to that group.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Groups tab">
    From your document's status page, click **Assign this file** or navigate to the **Groups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your groups">
    A table lists all available groups with their member count. Check the box next to each group that should receive this document.

    - Click **+ Add new group** if you need to create a new group first.
    - Click **Manage Groups** to review or edit group membership before assigning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Update** to save the group assignments. The button is disabled until you make a change.

    <Note>
      Group assignments can only be changed on the **latest version** of a document. Checkboxes are locked on older versions.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 8: Publish the document

Publishing makes the document live and automatically creates assignments for all users in the assigned groups.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the status page">
    Open the document's status page. Policy Passport shows a checklist of everything required before you can publish:

    - **File version set** — required
    - **File assigned to at least one group** — required
    - **PDF file uploaded** — required

    Any unmet requirements are highlighted with a warning.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Once all requirements are met, click **Publish Now**. The document goes live immediately.

    When a document is published:
    - An **assignment** is automatically created for every active user in the assigned groups.
    - If a previous version of this document exists, it is automatically **archived**.
    - Users who open the document will be able to read, acknowledge (if required), complete a test (if set), and mark it as done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Publishing cannot be undone from this screen. If you need to take a document offline after publishing, use the **Archive** option from the document settings.
</Warning>

---

## What users see after assignment

Once the document is published and assigned, each user in the selected groups will:

1. See the document appear in their **My Documents** list.
2. Be able to open and read the PDF inside Policy Passport.
3. Complete any required test, acknowledgement, or feedback you configured.
4. Mark the document as **complete** once they've finished.

If you set **Days to Complete**, each user's assignment will show a due date calculated from the date the document was published.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I assign a document to an individual user without a group?">
    No. Policy Passport assigns documents via groups only. To assign a document to one person, create a group with just that person in it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I add a user to a group after publishing?">
    New users added to an assigned group automatically receive an assignment for all published documents linked to that group. You do not need to republish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the assigned groups after publishing?">
    Yes. Open the **Groups** tab on the document, update your group selection, and click **Update**. Users in newly added groups will receive an assignment immediately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my PDF is over 25 MB?">
    Compress the PDF using a tool such as Adobe Acrobat, Smallpdf, or ILovePDF before uploading. Alternatively, split it into multiple smaller documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can users download the PDF?">
    By default, yes. To prevent downloading and printing, check **Disable Download and Printing** in Step 3.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
