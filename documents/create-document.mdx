Creating a document in Policy Passport is done through a guided multi-step wizard. This article walks you through each step so you can get your first document published with confidence.

## Before you start

Make sure you have administrator access to your Policy Passport dashboard. If you can't see the **Files** section in the sidebar, contact your account owner to check your permissions.

---

## Step 1: Name your document

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Files section">
    From your admin dashboard, click **Files** in the left sidebar, then click **New File** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a document name">
    Type a descriptive name for your document in the text field. The name appears in the preview panel on the left as you type.

    <Note>
      The **Next** button stays disabled until you enter a name. The name can be changed later from the document settings.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Next">
    Once you've entered a name, click **Next** to move on to content type selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 2: Choose a content type

Policy Passport supports five content types. Select the one that matches your document:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="File Upload" icon="file-pdf">
    Upload a **PDF** directly. Ideal for policies, procedures, and formal documents. Max file size: 25 MB.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video" icon="circle-play">
    Embed a **YouTube or Vimeo** video. Great for training or explainer content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Text" icon="pen-to-square">
    Compose content directly in Policy Passport using a rich text editor. Good for internal notes and policies you want to write from scratch.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Link" icon="link">
    Link to an external public URL. Useful for pointing users to a webpage or shared document.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  If your organisation has Employment Equity (EE) verification enabled, you'll also see an **EE Verification** option.
</Info>

### Uploading a PDF

Drag and drop your PDF onto the upload area, or click to browse for a file. A progress bar shows the upload status. Once the upload is complete, the **Next** button appears.

### Embedding a video

Select your video provider (**YouTube** or **Vimeo**), then paste the video ID or URL into the embed token field. A live preview of the video appears once a valid token is detected.

### Writing text content

Use the built-in Quill rich text editor to compose your content. Toggle between **Edit** and **Preview** modes using the button above the editor. Click **Save** before proceeding.

### Adding a link

Paste the full URL (including `https://`) into the URL field, then click **Next**.

## Step 3: Version and priority settings

This step captures metadata that controls how the document is managed and displayed.

| Field | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **File Version** | Yes | A version label (e.g. `1.0`, `2024-01`). Required before the document can be published. |
| **Category** | No | Assign the document to an existing category for organisation. |
| **Days to Complete** | No | Set a deadline (in days) for users to read and complete the document. |
| **Disable Download and Printing** | No | When checked, users can only view the document inside Policy Passport. |
| **Do Not Embed PDF** | No | Shows a download link instead of displaying the PDF inline (PDF uploads only). |
| **Lock Document** | No | Prevents other administrators from editing the document. |

<Tip>
  The **File Version** field is the only required field in this step. You won't be able to publish the document without it.
</Tip>

Click **Next** once you're happy with your settings.

---

## Step 4: Notes and review details

This step lets you add an internal note and set optional review and compliance metadata.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Display Note">
    Check **Display Note?** to add a note that users see before they read the document. This is useful for calling out important context or caveats.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Administrative Review Date">
    Set a date to remind yourself (or other admins) when this document is due for internal review. You can also enable **Advanced Reminders** to receive email nudges at 90, 60, 30, and 7 days before the review date, and then daily until actioned.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Document Owner / Approved By / Date Approved">
    Optional fields to record who owns the document, who approved it, and when it was approved. These details are visible to administrators.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CPD Details">
    If your organisation uses Continuing Professional Development (CPD) tracking, fill in all five CPD fields: **Name**, **Points**, **Hours**, **Accreditation Number**, and **Accreditation Body**. These fields work as a set — if you fill in one, you must fill in all of them.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Click **Next** to continue.

---

## Step 5: Add a test (optional)

You can attach a quiz to your document to verify that users have understood the content.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable testing">
    Select **Yes** to create a test, or **No** to skip this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of questions">
    Enter how many questions users must answer. This sets the minimum — you can add more questions than this number and Policy Passport will randomly select from them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your questions">
    After clicking **Next**, you'll be taken to the question builder where you can write each question and its answer options.

    <Tip>
      If your document is a PDF upload and your organisation has AI question generation enabled, you can click **Generate Questions Automatically** to have Policy Passport suggest questions based on the document content.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Step 6: Acknowledgement and feedback (optional)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Require feedback">
    Check **Feedback Required Upon Completion?** and enter an instruction prompt (e.g. *"Do you have any questions about this policy?"*). Users will see this prompt and can submit a free-text response after reading the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Require acknowledgement">
    Check **Acknowledgement Required Upon Completion?** to present users with a sign-off statement when they finish the document.

    A default acknowledgement text is provided — you can edit it using the rich text editor to match your organisation's language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and finish">
    Click **Save** to complete the wizard. Your document is now created and ready to be published.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## What happens next?

Your document is saved in **draft** status. Before users can see it, you need to publish it:

1. Open the document from the **Files** list.
2. Review the document details on the status page.
3. Click **Publish** to make the document live for your users.

<Note>
  If you skipped the **File Version** field earlier, you won't be able to publish. Go back to the document settings and add a version before publishing.
</Note>
