---
title: "How to sign in"
description: "Learn how to sign in to Policy Passport using your username, a one-time PIN, or single sign-on."
---

Policy Passport supports three sign-in methods: **one-time PIN (OTP)**, **password**, and **single sign-on (SSO)**. The method available to you depends on how your organisation has configured its account.

## Step 1: Enter your username

1. Navigate to your organisation's Policy Passport login page.
2. Enter your **username**, **email address**, or **mobile number** in the field provided.
3. Click **Next**.

<Note>
  If your organisation requires you to sign in with your username specifically,
  entering an email address or mobile number will return an error. Use your
  username instead.
</Note>

## Step 2: Complete sign-in

After clicking **Next**, Policy Passport identifies your account and presents the sign-in method configured for you.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="One-time PIN (OTP)">
    Most users sign in with a one-time PIN sent to their registered **mobile number** or **email address**.

    1. A 6-character code is sent to your mobile or email automatically.
    2. Enter the code in the field provided.
    3. Click **Sign in**.

    <Note>
      Codes are valid for **15 minutes**. The code is automatically uppercased — you don't need to worry about capitalisation.
    </Note>

    **Didn't receive your code?**

    Wait 30 seconds for the initial delivery attempt to complete. If the code hasn't arrived:

    - Check your **spam, junk, and filtered message folders**.
    - Click **Click here** to request a new code.
    - If your account has both a mobile number and email address on file, you can switch delivery method by clicking the alternative **Click here** link.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Password">
    Some accounts use a traditional password to sign in.

    1. Your username is pre-filled and cannot be changed on this screen. To sign in as a different user, click **Not you?** to return to the previous step.
    2. Enter your **password**.
    3. Optionally, tick **Remember me** to stay signed in on this device.
    4. Click **Log in**.

    **Forgot your password?**

    Click **Forgot password?** and follow the prompts to reset it via email.
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Single sign-on (SSO)">
    If your organisation uses an identity provider (IdP) such as Azure AD, Okta, or Google Workspace, you may be redirected automatically to your company's login page after entering your username.

    Complete authentication through your IdP as normal — you'll be signed in to Policy Passport automatically once your identity is verified.

    Contact your IT administrator if you have trouble with SSO sign-in.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Your username could not be found" | Double-check the username, email, or mobile number you entered. Contact your administrator if you're unsure which to use. |
| OTP code not received | Wait 30 seconds, check spam folders, then request a new code. |
| OTP code expired | Codes are valid for 15 minutes. Request a new code and sign in promptly. |
| Forgot password | Click **Forgot password?** on the password screen to receive a reset link by email. |
| SSO not working | Contact your IT administrator to check your IdP configuration. |
| Account locked or inactive | Contact your Policy Passport administrator to have your account reviewed. |
